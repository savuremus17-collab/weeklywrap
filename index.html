Frequently asked questions

How do I compress a PDF for free, online?

Open the "Compress PDF" tab, drop your file, pick a compression level (Much smaller / Balanced / Slightly smaller), and click the button. You'll see exactly how much smaller it got before you download.

How do I merge multiple PDFs into one?

In the "Merge PDFs" tab, upload your files, drag them into the order you want, then click "Merge the PDFs". You get a single document, ready to download.

How do I turn an image (JPG/PNG) into a PDF?

In the "Images → PDF" tab, upload one or more images — each becomes a page. You can reorder them by dragging before creating the document.

How do I export PDF pages as JPG images?

In the "PDF → JPG" tab, upload your file and every page is automatically converted to JPG, downloadable individually or as a .zip archive.

Does this upload my files to a server?

No. All processing happens locally, in your browser, using the pdf-lib and pdf.js libraries. Your files never leave your device.
