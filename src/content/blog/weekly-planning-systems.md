# Weekly Planning Systems for Maximum Productivity

Most people plan their day on Monday morning. By then, it's already too late. You're already reacting to emails and Slack pings.

To truly lead your business, you need a **Weekly Planning System.**

## The Sunday Ritual

High-performers treat Sunday evening as their "Strategy Session." Here's how to structure it.

### 1. Review the Previous Week
Look at your calendar and your tasks.
- What actually got done?
- Where did you lose time?
- **Pro Tip:** Use your WeeklyWrap report to see an objective summary of where your energy went.

### 2. Identify the "Big 3"
What are the three things that, if completed, would make the week a success? Write them down. Everything else is secondary.

### 3. Time Block the Deep Work
Before your calendar fills up with meetings, block out the time for your Big 3. Schedule them when you have the most energy (usually morning).

## The AI Planning Assistant

In 2025, you don't have to do this alone. AI can help you:
- **Prioritize:** "Based on my deadlines in Notion, what should I focus on this week?"
- **Optimize:** "Move all my internal meetings to Thursday afternoon to protect my morning focus time."
- **Summarize:** "Give me a one-paragraph summary of what I need to achieve this week to stay on track for my monthly goals."

## Stay Flexible

A plan is a compass, not a cage. If something urgent arises on Wednesday, adjust. But because you have a plan, you'll know exactly what you are sacrificing to handle that "emergency."

Plan your week. Build your business.
