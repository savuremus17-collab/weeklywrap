About Teanc

Teanc is a small set of free, browser-based PDF tools: turn images into a PDF, merge several PDFs into one, compress a PDF, or export its pages as JPG images.

The name comes from the Romanian word for "stack" — the pile of papers on your desk that this tool helps you put in order.

How it works

Every tool runs entirely inside your browser tab, using the open-source libraries pdf-lib and pdf.js. There's no server processing your files, no upload step, and no account to create — you drop a file in, and a moment later you download the result.

Why free

Because there's no file storage or server-side processing to pay for, running this site costs very little. It's meant to stay a fast, no-friction utility rather than a product with tiers or paywalls.
